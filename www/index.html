Azkar aur duain

الأذكار

Har dua ke neeche hawala likha hai. Sirf Quran aur hadith ki mustanad kutub (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami Tirmidhi) se liya gaya hai, kisi firqe ki khaas kitab se nahi. Hadith ke numbers sunnah.com ke mutabiq hain, chhapi hui kitabon mein numbering mukhtalif ho sakti hai. Phir bhi kisi aalim se ek baar tasdeeq kar lein.

Istemal ka tareeqa

  1. Apne phone ke compass app mein North dekhein.
  2. North se upar likhe angle ke baraber clockwise ghoomein.
  3. Jis taraf rukh ho wohi Qibla hai. Kisi masjid ki mehrab se bhi mila lein.

Is version mein live compass nahi hai, kyunke browser mein phone ka compass band hota hai. Shehar Namaz tab mein badlein.

Hijri tareekh

Masjid ki jamaat ka waqt

Apni masjid ka waqt likhein. Ya to pakka waqt likhein, ya yeh likhein ke namaz shuru hone ke kitne minute baad jamaat hoti hai. Maine andaaza se aam waqt rakhe hain (Zuhr 1:30 PM, Fajr 30, Asr 30, Maghrib 5, Isha 45 minute baad), apni masjid ke mutabiq badal lein. Dono khali kar dein to us namaz ka jamaat nahi dikhega.

Nazar

Deenora ke bare mein

Namaz ke auqat is device par hisaab se nikalte hain (Karachi / Umm al-Qura tareeqa), is liye internet ke baghair bhi chalte hain. Apni masjid ke time-table se ek do minute ka farq ho sakta hai, isay minute adjust se theek karein.
